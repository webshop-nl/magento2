## Feed Structure (`feed/feed-structure.spec.ts`)

The shape of the file the channel reads: newline-delimited JSON, one product per line, every line parseable, every product identifiable. A single unparseable line makes the whole file unreadable to the channel, so this is checked before anything about the contents.

## Multi Store (`feed/multi-store.spec.ts`)

Until release 1.2.0 a shop with more than one store view got one feed. The price collection was built once and reused, so the second store view added the same price_index join a second time and the run stopped on "You cannot define a correlation name 'price_index' more than once" - after the first file was already on disk, which is why it read as a success. The second store view is created in global setup, so this runs on a shop that ships one.

19. Every store view › one run writes a feed for every enabled store view
20. Every store view › the run reports every store view and no collection error
21. Every store view › each store view writes its own products, not the first one twice

## Parent Price (`feed/parent-price.spec.ts`)

The price of a configurable parent: the price a customer can actually pay for the cheapest variant, which is what the store page shows. Neither column of Magento's price index answers that on its own. min_price can sit below every variant - with a catalog price rule on a configurable, a 35.00 product indexes as final 28.00 and min 21.00 while all variants cost 28.00. final_price can sit above them - with the discount on the variants instead, the same product indexes as final 35.00 while the cheapest variant costs 28.00. Both published a price nothing in the catalogue sells for, which is what this spec exists to keep out of the feed.

22. Configurable parent price › the parent is published at the price of its cheapest variant
23. Configurable parent price › the parent price is one a variant actually sells for
24. Configurable parent price › neither index column is published blind
25. Configurable parent price › every variant carries its own price, not the one next to it in the batch
26. Configurable parent price › both prices are written as an amount with a currency

## Product Selection (`feed/product-selection.spec.ts`)

Which products reach the channel. Until release 1.2.0 the "exclude out of stock" setting filled in an unrelated "add disabled products" flag, so with the setting off every disabled product was published and no product was ever held back for being out of stock.

27. Product selection › a disabled product stays out of the feed
28. Product selection › with the stock filter on, an out of stock product stays out of the feed
29. Product selection › a variant carries its parent in family_ID and a standalone product does not

## Product URL (`feed/product-url.spec.ts`)

Until release 1.2.0 the feed shipped 1859 of its 2040 products without a link: only a product with a URL rewrite of its own got one, so every variant of a configurable and every component of a bundle reached the channel with an empty productURL and nothing to click through to. A product without a link is not sellable through the channel, which makes this the one field that must never be empty again.

30. Product URL › every product has a non-empty productURL
31. Product URL › every productURL is an absolute http(s) address
32. Product URL › a variant of a configurable has a link of its own
